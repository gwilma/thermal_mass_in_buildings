Rules of thumb

  • Mass stores nothing unless its temperature changes. It needs capacity, access and a swing.
  • Room air stores almost nothing: a 30 m² office's air is about 1/70 of the top 100 mm of its concrete soffit.
  • The surface film limits charging: 1/Rsi ≈ 7.7 W/m²K, so 3 K drives only 23 W/m².
  • In well-insulated buildings, ventilation and surface exchange dwarf fabric conduction.
  • 1 kWh = 3.6 MJ.
