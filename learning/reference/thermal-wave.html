The thermal wave

How far a periodic swing gets into a material, and how much heat it carries.

Rules of thumb

  • At 1 δ: 37% of the swing, 3.8 h late (daily). At 3 δ: 5%.
  • δ grows with √T: a week reaches 2.65 times deeper than a day.
  • Good mass needs high α and high b. Insulation has high α but negligible b.
  • Insulation-backed layer, daily cycle: storage peaks at about 0.7–1 δ (dense concrete 100–110 mm, 90% by about 55 mm; CLT about 75 mm).
  • Thick concrete could take 18.7 W/m²K, but the air film allows only 7.7. The surface is the bottleneck.
