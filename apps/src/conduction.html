Conduction Lab

Drive one element with a hot-day room temperature and watch heat soak in. A fine finite-difference mesh is the reference; the ISO 52016 / HEM five-node lump runs alongside so you can see where it gains or loses storage.

Room air temperature (sinusoid)

Runs four days with 5-minute implicit steps and reports the last day. Outdoor-facing elements see the far-side temperature through Rse; internal elements see it directly or through Rsi.
